隐私政策

最后更新：2026-11-16

一句话：Liteshot 不联网，不收集任何数据。截图与文字识别全部在你的 Mac 上完成，没有任何内容会离开这台电脑。

我们不收集什么

  • 不收集任何个人信息、账号信息或设备标识
  • 不包含统计、分析、崩溃上报或广告 SDK
  • 不发起任何网络请求（应用内没有联网代码）
  • 不读取、上传或分析你的截图内容

数据如何被处理

截图、标注、滚动拼接、二维码识别都在本机内存中完成。文字识别（OCR）调用 macOS 内置的 Vision 框架，同样在本机运行。生成的图片只会写入你在「设置」里指定的本地文件夹。

截图历史保存在本机的应用数据目录内，最多保留 50 张，可在应用内清空。

系统权限

屏幕录制：仅用于截图功能。首次使用时应用会先显示自己的说明页，再由你决定是否在「系统设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 屏幕与系统音频录制」中授权。你可以随时在那里撤销。

文件夹访问：仅用于把图片写入你选择的保存目录（默认是「图片」或「下载」下的 Liteshot 文件夹）。

第三方服务

没有。应用不集成任何第三方 SDK，也不与任何服务器通信。

儿童隐私

应用不收集任何人的数据，自然也不收集儿童的数据。

政策变更

如果将来隐私实践有变化，本页会更新并修改上方日期；重大变化会在应用更新说明中提示。

联系我们

任何隐私相关问题，发邮件到 carl030101@icloud.com。

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

In one sentence: Liteshot never goes online and collects no data. Screenshots and text recognition are processed entirely on your Mac. Nothing leaves the machine.

What we don't collect

  • No personal information, account information, or device identifiers
  • No analytics, crash reporting, or advertising SDKs
  • No network requests at all — the app contains no networking code
  • No reading, uploading, or analyzing of your screenshots

How data is processed

Capture, annotation, scrolling stitch, and QR detection all happen in memory on your Mac. Text recognition (OCR) uses Apple's built-in Vision framework and runs on-device as well. Generated images are written only to the local folder you choose in Settings.

Capture history is stored in the app's local data folder, capped at 50 items, and can be cleared from inside the app.

System permissions

Screen Recording: used only for capturing the screen. The app does not trigger the system prompt on launch — it shows its own explanation first, and you decide in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen & System Audio Recording. You can revoke it there at any time.

Folder access: used only to write images into the save folder you pick (by default a Liteshot folder inside Pictures or Downloads).

Third-party services

None. The app integrates no third-party SDKs and talks to no servers.

Children's privacy

The app collects no data from anyone, children included.

Changes to this policy

If our privacy practices change, this page will be updated along with the date above; significant changes will be called out in the app's release notes.
